This project detects parking availability and sends SMS alerts when spaces become available.

SMS Program Overview

Purpose: Notify subscribed users when parking becomes available.

Opt‑In: Users opt in by submitting their phone number through the signup form or requesting notifications.

Message Frequency: Message frequency varies depending on parking availability.

Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply.

Opt‑Out: Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Help: Reply HELP for assistance.
